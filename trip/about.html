About Travel Journey Sharing

Travel Journey Sharing is a platform where travelers can share their adventures, upload photos, videos, and GPX tracks, and connect with fellow explorers.

Features include:

  • Creating and sharing travel journeys
  • Uploading multimedia content
  • Interactive maps with GPX tracks
  • Community comments and likes
  • User-friendly dashboard
